**Generate the song. Keep working in your DAW.** Plenio VST3 brings the
[Plenio Music Production System](https://github.com/jplenio/Plenio-Music-Production-System) into REAPER, Cubase,
Studio One, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Bitwig and every other DAW that loads VST3 instruments: describe a song or pick a
style, and Plenio writes, composes, sings and plays it on your own graphics card. Every take lands on your track, in
time with your project, with its stems, its MIDI and a tempo map that fits.

## What it needs

- A DAW that loads **VST3** instruments (Logic Pro and Pro Tools do not).
- To make songs: **ComfyUI** with **Plenio 1.6** or newer and its models, on a graphics card that runs them (an
  older Plenio makes songs too, without stems and measured bars). Plenio VST3 finds ComfyUI by itself and can start it.
- Windows: the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (part of Windows 11; the installer checks).

## What is in it

- A song or a cover in your DAW: up to **four takes per run**, all on the same track, each with **stems**, cover art,
  lyrics, melody and chords.
- **Change it and render again**: the words, the melody, the chords, the brief. Every run adds takes, also when the song
  gets longer or shorter.
- **In time with your project**: the chosen take plays from bar 1; stems on outputs of their own; melody, instrument line
  and chords as MIDI out.
- **Drag it in**: the take and stems as WAV, the score as MIDI, the **tempo map of the measured bars**, a click.
- **REAPER**: *Put on tracks* lays the whole song out on the project's tracks, every take as one of REAPER's takes.
- **Plenio ARA** on a clip: the take's tempo, bars, key, notes and chords for the host; another take in the same clip.
- **Every DAW's own steps** in the plug-in itself.
- **Offline**: a saved project plays and edits without ComfyUI; only *Generate* needs it.

## Known limits of this alpha

- macOS is experimental (see above); Linux comes later (the core already runs there).
- The installers are not code-signed: Windows SmartScreen may ask (*More info › Run anyway*), macOS wants a
  right-click › *Open* on the `.pkg` the first time.
- Plenio ARA's window lists the takes; the lyrics, notes and chords in that window come later.
